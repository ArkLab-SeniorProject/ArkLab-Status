Research Aid APIs for Eligibility & Application Guidance​

Mack — 2 hrs

  • Due Date 10/15/2026
  • Description: From a design perspective we want to understand what data the disaster aid APIs we're considering (e.g., OpenFEMA) actually provide, and whether we can use it to tell users what they might qualify for and how applying works. This will help inform our filtering system as well as our intake questionnaire.
  • Deliverable: Aftermath: Avenues for Algorithmic Eligibility & Document Matching

Takeaways

  • Our API's can only filter so much, we will need to build a mapping system to connect our filters to our API.
  • Some other government sites have information publicly available on how they match people with resources, can scrape for starting point.

Create a Tone of Voice Document​

Mack — 2 hrs

  • Due Date 10/18/2026
  • Description: Develop a tone of voice document to guide the way our content and copy should sound across all channels.
  • Deliverable: Tone of Voice Document

Takeaways

  • Aftermath is meant to sound warm, guiding and confident but not to the level of wellness or therapy.
  • When it comes to utilizing a tone of voice document for AI, it's important to be very clear and specific about how you do and do not want to sound like and to give specific examples, model matters.

Monday, Oct. 26 [3:00-5:00pm]​

Design and Research Team (Maple, Amy, and Mack)

Meeting Agenda​

  • Purpose
    • Use the lo-fi prototype to validate the critical flow path and check layouts before f- Finalizing designs
    • Focus on flow and intuitiveness, not colors or content (those get tested in Mid-Fi)
    • Usability interview is scheduled for tomorrow; Maple will take notes
  • Scope and Test Structure
  • Test only the core flows because there are few participants: recovery intake, dashboard, and nav bar items (recovery, prepare, map, profile)
    • Skip guest flow and account creation; participants are logged-in subject matter experts, not disaster survivors
    • Test the recovery flow in the FEMA category only, ending at financial assistance and then a guide
    • Test sections as separate flows rather than one continuous run, with screens segmented so participants still understand what comes next
    • Participants should test on a laptop; later developed versions can be sent as links
  • Dashboard A/B Testing
    • Two dashboard versions (V1 and V2), with only one feature changed
    • Participants complete the intake flow once, then view both dashboards individually and say which they prefer to land on
    • Avoid having participants repeat the full process twice, since it would feel too long
  • Preparedness and Map
    • Preparedness questions duplicate the recovery intake questions, so the plan should be pre-populated
    • Decision: instead of locking the Prepare tab, show a pop-up explaining the preparedness plan was generated from earlier answers, then unlock it
  • Map is a nice-to-have; participants reach it from preparedness (e.g., finding local hospitals)
  • Profile / My Situation
    • Keep it labeled “Profile” for now since users can edit intake answers there; revisit if participants find it confusing
    • Ask generally where they’d go to change earlier answers, rather than asking about filters directly
    • Filter screen isn’t built; add placeholder/filler screens so participants can react to it
  • Questions to Cover
    • Do the intake questions have any gaps or confusing wording?
    • Do participants dislike slide-up mechanics? Does the slide-up expand to full screen?
    • What do they expect to see under filters or situation in the profile?
    • Add a few quantitative metrics (e.g., 1–5 understanding rating) alongside qualitative notes
  • Action Items
    • Amy: Draft the test script and refine questions tonight; add gray boxes and placeholder screens; review the plan later tonight
    • Mack: Set up the notes document and the usability plan, structured by flow; send the call/chat message asking if anything was missed
    • Maple: Take notes during the interview and ask questions as needed
